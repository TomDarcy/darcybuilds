Case study / FactoryPulse

Factory measures, projects
and problems in one place.

I built FactoryPulse for a client to bring factory measures, improvement projects and problem-solving workflows into one tool. Teams could organise information around their factory, department and cell, then use it in day-to-day operations.

The manufacturing problem

Keep the measures connected to the work.

An operations team needs to review its measures, follow improvement projects and work through production problems. FactoryPulse brought those workflows together so the team could see the information alongside the actions it needed to take.

Information could be organised by factory, department and cell, giving teams a consistent way to work with their data.

Inside the tool

Tools around the operational workflow

Teams could configure boards around their measures, track improvement projects by stage and owner, and work through problems using a defined template.

The outcome

A shared view of the work to follow up.

FactoryPulse gave the client a place to organise operational measures, improvement projects and structured problem records by team. Boards showed what needed attention; project owners and problem-solving templates helped the team follow up.
