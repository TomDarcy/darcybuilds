Manufacturing experience, applied to the build

The expensive part isn’t writing the code.

A developer can build to a specification. The work before that is deciding what the specification needs to say: how a revised job affects material demand, who can change a BoM, how purchasing sees shortages and what an operator can realistically enter during a shift.

I start in the factory with the people doing the work. We map the normal process and the exceptions, agree what stays in your ERP and identify the smallest application that will help. I then build it and review it with the same team.

What about Power Apps or your IT supplier?

They may be the right route. If your existing tools and supplier can handle the workflow well, I’ll say so. A lower-cost developer can also be a good choice when you already have a clear specification and someone to own the operational decisions.

Darcy Builds brings manufacturing experience, process diagnosis and the person writing the software together. You work directly with me from the factory visit through to handover.

Ownership & handover

Your application isn’t held hostage.

You own your data. The application should remain useful whether you keep working with me, bring someone in-house or ask another developer to take it forward.

Your data stays yours

We agree how your team accesses the data and exports it in a usable format. At handover, you know where it lives and how it is backed up, so moving it or passing it to another developer doesn’t depend on knowledge only I have.

Source code is agreed up front

The proposal sets out ownership, licensing and access to the project’s source code. Any third-party components and their licence terms are identified. You know what you’re getting before the build starts, and the agreed code access is part of the handover.

Clear hosting arrangements

Where practical, hosting and service accounts sit with your business. If I help manage them, we agree who has access, who pays the ongoing costs and how control can be transferred. Keeping the application running doesn’t require a development retainer.

A handover another developer can use

You receive documentation covering setup, deployment, the data structure, integrations and routine maintenance. We walk through the application with your team and arrange secure access to the relevant accounts. Another competent developer can take over with the code, documentation and access needed to run and develop it.

A few practical questions

Before you send the spreadsheet

Do I need to replace my ERP?

No. The application sits around the existing systems. We can use spreadsheet imports, ERP exports or an agreed integration, depending on what the workflow needs.

Is this right for my business?

The best fit is a UK manufacturer with roughly 20–250 people, established systems and one awkward operational process outside them. I work directly with MDs, operations leaders and engineering teams.

What if an off-the-shelf tool would do?

I’ll say so. The initial discussion is free. We start with the operational problem and choose an existing tool when it meets the team’s needs.

Can I start without sharing the whole workbook?

Yes. Screenshots with sensitive details removed or a two-minute description are enough for the first conversation. We can agree how to share the detail later.

What happens after the sprint?

You get documentation and a handover, with support responsibilities agreed in advance. Another workflow can become another fixed-price sprint. If the work reveals a more substantial project, we scope and price that separately.
